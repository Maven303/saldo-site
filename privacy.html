Privacy policy · Pravila privatnosti

Saldo desktop app · last updated 10 October 2026

Your data stays on your computer

Saldo stores everything you enter (months, entries, budgets, goals, wishlist, settings) in a local database on your own Windows computer. The developer runs no servers, collects no analytics and receives none of your data.

Google Drive backups (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Saldo uses Google sign-in in your browser and requests only the drive.file permission. With it Saldo can create a folder named “Saldo” in your Drive and upload, list, download and delete the backup files it created there. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

Backup files are copies of your Saldo database and go directly from your computer to your Google account. The sign-in token is stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows (DPAPI). Saldo also reads your Google account’s email address and name to show which account is connected.

You can disconnect at any time in Saldo (Data → Backups → Disconnect), which revokes Saldo’s access, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Saldo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the backup feature to you, is not transferred to anyone else, and is not used for advertising.

Podaci ostaju na vašem računalu

Saldo sve što upišete (mjesece, stavke, budžete, ciljeve, listu želja, postavke) sprema u lokalnu bazu na vašem računalu. Razvojni programer nema poslužitelje, ne prikuplja analitiku i ne prima vaše podatke.

Sigurnosne kopije na Google Drive (po želji)

Ako povežete Google Drive, Saldo koristi Google prijavu u pregledniku i traži samo dopuštenje drive.file. Njime može stvoriti mapu „Saldo” na vašem Driveu te u njoj spremati, prikazivati, preuzimati i brisati kopije koje je sam stvorio. Ostale datoteke na vašem Driveu ne vidi i ne mijenja.

Kopije su preslike vaše Saldo baze i idu izravno s vašeg računala na vaš Google račun. Prijava se čuva samo na vašem računalu, šifrirana Windowsima (DPAPI). Saldo čita i e-adresu i ime Google računa da prikaže koji je račun povezan.

Vezu možete prekinuti u Saldu (Podaci → Sigurnosne kopije → Odspoji), čime se pristup opoziva, ili na myaccount.google.com/permissions. Kopije koje su već na Driveu ostaju dok ih ne obrišete.
